SG Sports administration

Set up and manage this backend: sign-in, TV pairing, IPTV sources, favorites and backups. Secrets are write-only: they are stored by the backend and never shown again. The TV app shows your games; this page is only for configuration.

                    Artwork (optional)

                    This release ships no team or league logos. Your backend fetches them for you, keeps them in its data volume and serves them to your paired TVs; a team without a logo shows a colour monogram. Retrieval at runtime is not a licence to redistribute the images, so nothing here is copied into the app or the repository.
